# Taste review: Open Terminal v2 acceptance screenshots

Reviewed by eye only, against the Instinct bar (dark UI, three equal columns of chart widgets each with a title and a one-line description, legend chips inside the plot, last-value badges on the axis, a chat panel whose reply cites widgets). Inputs: all twelve `accept-agent-*` cases at 1440x900 and 1920x1080, all six `accept-v2-*` at 1440 (rates-week, tsla-earnings, tsla-puts also at 1920), and eight `accept-v1-*` at 1440 (earnings-setup and vague also at 1920). Data correctness was not graded.

## Verdict

**5 / 10 against Instinct.** The vocabulary is right and the bones are good: a dark, quiet shell; tabular mono numerals everywhere they matter; legend chips with last value and change inside the plot; coloured last-value badges on the price axis; title plus one-line sub-header on every widget; chips in the reply; a "Worked for 5.6 s · 7 steps" line. Three widgets in the set would pass at Instinct unchanged (the Mag 7 correlation heatmap, the TSLA puts chain, the "Implied earnings move" card). But almost no *screen* passes. The grid is two columns, not three, at both viewports, and at 1920 it widens the two columns instead of adding one, so stat rows leave 60 % of their width empty and tables fling their four columns across 1,500 px. The packer gives one-row quote and facts cards a fixed slot, so a quote renders at 90 px full-width in one case and a facts card clips its first and last rows under the header in three others. Every chart with more than four series puts a stacked legend over the top-left third of the plot with the lines drawn *behind* it, uses a palette that repeats green three times at seven series, and lets badges collide (71.86/71.08, 100.94/100.69, 537.14/528.75). Raw identifiers leak into the UI (`eps_history`, `spread_10y_2y`, `after-hours`, `not-supplied`, headers `pair`, `entry`/`Entry`, `num estimates`). The widget that answers the question is below the fold in four of twelve cases (neoclouds' compare table, rates-week's calendar, spy-backtest's trades, crwv's bonds). And the reply, which is often the best-written part of the screen, shows the user a red "Not in any frame: 2.6%, 25.50, 12.10…" diagnostic and puts chips mid-sentence followed by a stranded full stop. None of these is a hard fix; together they are the difference between "a terminal" and "a dashboard builder that has read about terminals."

## Ranked changes (fifteen)

1. **Packer: three equal columns, content-sized heights.** Three columns at 1440 with the panel open and at 1920 (today it is two, and 1920 just widens them). Chart height capped at ~0.6 × width so no time series is ever portrait; stat, facts and one-row quote widgets are one row tall and never full width; a table that is the answer (compare, calendar, trades) ranks above a stat row. Affects every case; worst in neoclouds, spy-backtest, crwv, rates-week, earnings-setup, nvda-week. Component: `packLayout`. **M.**
2. **Stat row: compact card as the default.** The 80 px card from `morning` (ticker · label / 28 px number / change) everywhere; the row is a flex grid that fills or centres, never orphans one card; the one-row quote widget caps its hero at 32 px with fields in a 3 × 2 grid. Affects crwv, neoclouds, spx, amd-rsi, tsla-puts, aapl-today, mag7, vague, movers. Component: stat/quote view. **S.**
3. **Facts card typography and sizing.** One hero number per card; every other field is label : value at body size in a two- or three-column grid; dates, enums and strings never at display size; values shrink-to-fit rather than wrap; the card's height comes from its content so nothing renders under the header or clips at the foot. Affects tsla-earnings (both), nvda-pe, tsla-puts, amd-rsi, curve. Component: facts view, packer row height. **M.**
4. **Multi-series charts: legend, palette, badges.** Legend on one row wrapping to at most two, or a right-hand column when series > 4, with the plot inset below it so lines never pass behind it; a seven-hue palette with no repeated greens or oranges; badges within 12 px of each other collapse to one or stack with a leader; the badge hides the tick label it covers. Affects cheap-tech, morning, mag7-corr, mag7, semis, earnings-setup, crwv, neoclouds, spy-backtest, rates-week, curve. Component: chart legend, palette, axis badge. **M.**
5. **Tables: size columns to content, align by type.** Columns take their content width with a max, left-packed, never justified across the widget; every numeric column right-aligned in one format (no `1.94K` beside `2,310`); every cell vertically centred (Session, status sit top-aligned); headers in sentence case from display names, never raw (`pair`, `date`, `session`, `entry`/`Entry`, `num estimates`, `metric`, `day`). Affects mag7-corr, spx, nvda-pe, crwv, morning, rates-week, spy-backtest, earnings-setup, semis. Component: table control. **M.**
6. **Best/worst marking only where it means something.** Mark the comparative or signed columns the prompt asked about; never fill an absolute column (EPS, EPS est., Close, Mkt cap); use a 2 px left bar or bold weight instead of a full-cell fill; never fill *and* colour the text. Affects tsla-earnings, cheap-tech, earnings-setup, vague. Component: table cell renderer. **S.**
7. **Red and green restraint.** Colour only signed changes; a column negative by construction ("vs 52w high", "Move to BE %", put deltas) renders neutral; the legend's change figure is a muted tint of the series colour, not red/green (red `-28.14%` next to a red Nebius line says the same thing twice); "(2 failed)", "(1 failed)" and the unverified-figures list are not red. Affects cheap-tech, tsla-puts, crwv, rates-week, spx, neoclouds, semis, vague. Component: legend chip, table cell, run header. **S.**
8. **Verification display.** Replace the red "Not in any frame: 2.6%, 25.50 …" line with the muted "29 of 35 figures verified" footer plus a dotted underline on each unverified figure in the text with a hover; the list belongs in the run log. Affects spx, neoclouds, any failing case. Component: reply panel. **S.**
9. **Chips: one placement rule.** Chips sit at the end of the paragraph they support, never mid-sentence; nothing follows a chip on its line; the reply scroll area reserves the composer's height so the chip row and footer are never hidden behind it; chip 18 px tall, 11 px text. Affects spx (v2), vague, tsla-earnings (v2), tsla-puts, earnings-setup, movers. Component: reply markdown renderer. **S.**
10. **Reply length and voice.** Lead with the answer in one sentence; three short paragraphs for a single-subject prompt; large counts rounded (95 M shares, 1,027 % D/E); no system talk ("on yfinance", "the dashboard holds at most eight widgets", a paragraph on provider disagreement). Affects crwv, neoclouds, tsla-puts, tsla-earnings (v2), mag7, cheap-tech. Component: agent prompt. **S.**
11. **No raw identifiers in the UI.** Series labels, legend chips, sub-headers and field labels come from display names: "Earnings" not `eps_history`, "10Y−2Y" not `spread_10y_2y`, "After the close" not `after-hours`, "—" not `not-supplied`, "Estimates" not `num estimates`; sub-headers read "TSLA · Oct 23 puts · 24 rows" rather than `options · TSLA · 2026-10-23 · 12 · put · 24 rows`. Affects tsla-earnings, nvda-pe, rates-week, curve, morning, tsla-puts, earnings-setup, options-nvda. Component: widget header, legend, facts labels. **S.**
12. **Event markers anchored to the bar.** Marker sits above the bar's high with a short stem, never at a fixed y; the plot is inset so a marker on the first bar is not clipped; markers on the same bar dedupe; 14 px circle with the letter inside; the marker legend chip reads "Earnings"/"News" with the icon. Affects spx, tsla-earnings, nvda-pe, aapl-today, nvda-week, tsla-puts. Component: chart markers layer. **M.**
13. **Axis legibility.** Index levels as `7,659` not `7.66K` and the legend's last value at full precision; the last x tick always fits (`30Y`, not `30'`); the TradingView mark moves to the widget footer so it stops colliding with the MACD histogram and volume bars; one decimal rule per axis. Affects spx, curve, rates-week, amd-rsi, options-nvda, every chart. Component: chart axis formatter. **S.**
14. **Three presets.** OI and volume by strike as paired bars with the underlying as a vertical rule; yield curve as dots-with-line on a categorical tenor axis; "last week" shading as a faint band with a 1 px edge and candle width capped (~14 px) so 20 bars do not render as slabs. Affects tsla-puts (both), options-nvda, curve, rates-week, spx (both), nvda-week. Component: presets. **M.**
15. **Shell chrome.** Tabs truncate to "Memory …", "Mag 7 In…", "TSLA pu…" at ~80 px: give tabs a 140 px minimum with the subject ticker first and overflow the rest; drop "22 kinds" from the status bar (internal); keep the ticker strip, it is right. Affects every screen. Component: tab bar, status bar. **S.**

## The three that move the score most

1. **The packer (1).** Three columns and content-sized heights fix, in one change, the empty stat rows, the portrait charts, the clipped cards and the answer-below-the-fold problem. It is the gap between this and Instinct's grid, and it is the first thing the eye reads on every screen.
2. **Card typography (2 + 3).** Compact stats and one-hero facts cards turn the loudest, emptiest widgets on the page into quiet supporting ones, and stop the clipping that currently reads as a bug on four screens.
3. **Multi-series chart hygiene (4).** Legend off the data, a seven-hue palette and non-colliding badges make the comparison charts, which carry half the cases, legible instead of decorative.

With those three the set would sit at 7; tables (5, 6) and the reply fixes (8, 9, 10) take it to 8.
