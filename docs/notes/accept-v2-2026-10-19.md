# v2 acceptance loop (phase 2, 2026-10-19)

The loop PLAN.md section 17 asks for: build each of the twelve visual cases, screenshot it at 1440x900 and 1920x1080 with the conversation panel open, run the checklist in the browser, fix what fails, repeat. The driver is `scripts/accept.mjs` (screenshots plus the checklist, one JSON line per case and viewport and a Markdown table at the end); the bench prompts for the cases are the `v2-accept` group of `scripts/prompts.json`. The server for every round is the branch build on `127.0.0.1:4270` over a copy of `~/.terminal` (`TERMINAL_HOME` under the scratchpad; the JSON store migrated to SQLite on first start: 8 workspaces, 37 widgets), OpenBB 5.0.0 on 6900, Opus 5.5 configured.

Checklist columns (the section 17 list, one letter each in the tables): overflow (no element with `scrollWidth > clientWidth`), holes (no empty grid cell; a cell showing "No data", "Could not load" or a skeleton counts as empty), fill (content at least 60% of a table's or card's body, 80% of a chart's), oneRow (no table with one row), columns (three at 1440 with the panel open), headers (title and sub-header on every widget), charts (legend and last-value label on every chart), clipped (no text clipped mid-word: an ellipsis on text wider than its box, or a text box cut by `overflow: hidden`), verified (the reply's verification has no unverified number), chips (at least one chip, every chip names a widget), plain (no markdown headings, bullets or bold in the reply).

## Round 0: the agent is blocked

`BASE=http://127.0.0.1:4270 node scripts/prompt-bench.mjs --group v2-accept` failed every case in under a second with the Anthropic API's `Your credit balance is too low to access the Anthropic API`. The only LLM credential on the machine is `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in `~/terminal/.env` (no OpenAI key, no key in `config.yml`), so no prompt can reach the model until the account is topped up. The rubric half of the loop (answer first, facts complete, numbers verified, right widgets, cites, time window) therefore cannot be graded in this PR; the rendering half can.

What the loop still exercises without the agent: `scripts/accept-cases.mjs` holds the twelve workspaces hand-built from the case descriptions (the recipes, routes and transforms the prompt rules tell the agent to use: the P/E example verbatim, `ot.rsi`/`ot.macd`, `ot.corr`, FINRA bond routes, the screener route, the SMA backtest), and `node scripts/accept.mjs --build` creates them on the server and scores them with the same checklist (the three reply columns read n/a). Every rendering defect found this way is one the agent's workspaces would have shown too.

## Data-source limits, stated

- Case 12, "since 2020": the `ohlcv` recipe's range enum ends at `5y`, so the backtest runs from 2021-10. Clearing it: a `start_date` param on `ohlcv` (the OpenBB and yahoo routes both take one), phase 3.

Clearing it: a sector field on the screener rows (`equity/profile` per name is 60 calls) or a sector filter per call, phase 3.
- Case 10, "reporting today": no watchlist name reported on 2026-10-19, so the markers are the week's reports (TSLA on Oct 21) over the TSLA chart, read from the earnings recipe for the watchlist.
- Case 2, "no table under seven rows": the metrics pivot has twelve rows (metrics by symbol); the balance-sheet and estimates tables are five rows (one per neocloud) because five companies are five rows, and a five-row table is what a comparison of five names is; the packer gives them one row each and they fill it.
- Case 4, "IV term structure": the `options` recipe keeps one expiration (its contract), so the curve is IV by strike for Oct 23; a term structure needs the chain across expirations, which the raw cboe route serves and a transform can pivot (`strike` fixed, `expiration` on x), phase 3 once the agent can be run.

A second critic (`docs/notes/taste-v2-round2-2026-10-19.md`) scored the set 6 of 10 against Instinct and ranked ten changes. What changed, by the review's numbers:

1. **The packer fills the page.** Grid rows are tracks, two per row of a size; a stat, a facts card and a short table take one track, so two cards share a chart's height. Tracks are sized: one only cards occupy is as tall as their measured content, the others share the rest of the pane, every widget keeps a minimum (a compare or oscillator chart 300px, so a 160px plot; a long table eight rows). The packer picks among placement orders and span alternatives by a px cost (scroll, blank space in cards, portrait charts, the hero under 60% of the height, the first three widgets below the fold, no three-across track). A table narrower than a column takes one column. Every three- and four-widget screen now ends within 3px of the bottom at both viewports (it left 300px); five- and six-widget screens hold more than a viewport and scroll.
2. **Cards size to their content.** Hero, 8px, the fields; label beside value in pairs of 140 to 200px; labels on one line (shortened, or two pairs wide); a card is never wider than one column; nothing clips. A card that shares a track with a taller neighbour takes a little of the slack (its gaps grow by at most 20px), never the round 1 split.
3. **At most three badges per chart**, on the price pane; past three series the legend carries the values; none on a strike axis (totals and the at-the-money reading in the legend) or an indicator pane (its inset chip reads the value); a leader when a badge is stacked off its value; the tick a badge covers is blanked by the axis formatter. A rebased chart plots percent change, so the axis, the badge and the legend's change read one number.
4. **Candle legend**: a two-tone glyph; the change coloured by sign for one price series and neutral otherwise; IV's change neutral.
5. **One snapshot**: the widgets a run sets are served the frames the run fetched, and when the reply is done those frames are held (`meta.heldAt`, "as of 16:55" in the header) until the user refreshes a widget or clicks "Live data" in the footer, the same trading day; the footer says "verified against the data as of 16:55 ET · the widgets show this data" or "· the widgets show live data".
8. **Internal strings**: no "2 datasets"; "S&P 500" for `^GSPC`; "release markers" for a transform's markers; flags as ticks and "Conv."; "CRWV 9.25% 2030" for a FINRA bond; tab titles never repeat the ticker; no restored "Untitled" tab; the status bar says "after-hours" once.

The bugs outside the list: the hand-built P/E case marks `pe` as its key (it plotted the close); a table's signed move ranks right after the first column (crwv's "Last week %" was shed at 1440); two or three rows are cards only side by side (Memory trade's earnings cards opened scrolled into the middle of one; they are a table now) and the earnings recipe's yahoo fallback answers in the calendar's names; the hand-built tsla-puts volatility card already showed the IV and HV ranges and IV rank (checked at both viewports).

The checklist's fill measure now runs from the header to the frame's foot, not to the end of the body element: a content-sized card's body is its content, and blank space under it inside the frame must still count.
